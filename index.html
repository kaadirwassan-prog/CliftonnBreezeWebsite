Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Clifton Breeze Guest House located?

We have guest houses in two areas of Karachi: Clifton and DHA (Defence). Choose your preferred area when you book and we will send the exact location pin on WhatsApp.

How do I book a room?

Tap Book Now, choose your check-in and check-out dates and number of guests, and send the request. It reaches our booking desk on WhatsApp and we reply with availability and the rate. You can also call +92 300 0225347.

Do you have rooms for families?

Yes. Our family rooms have a double bed plus a single bed and a sitting area, and larger groups can book more than one room.

What is included in the rooms?

Rooms are air-conditioned with an attached bathroom, TV and WiFi. Selected rooms also have a mini fridge and a sitting area.

What time can I check in?

Our front desk is open 24 hours. Tell us your arrival time in the booking request and we will have the room ready.

What do I need to bring at check-in?

Please bring an original CNIC or passport for every adult guest.
